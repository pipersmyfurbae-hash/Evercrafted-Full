Draft Mode Note
Add --draft to any prompt for ~10× speed, ~1/10 cost — great for rapid palette and composition exploration before committing to full-quality renders. Draft mode is incompatible with --oref and Conversational Mode. Use draft to find your winner, then rerun at full quality without --draft.
